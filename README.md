### A little about me

Salut! I'm **ralf1q**, a developer working across games, websites, and automation. I enjoy turning ideas into practical experiences—whether that's a gameplay system, a community tool, or an application that makes everyday work easier.

My work brings together **Luau, Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript**, with a focus on connecting interfaces, services, and workflows.
